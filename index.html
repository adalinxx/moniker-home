One name, one page, yours to keep

An @handle

Lowercase letters, digits and _. Whoever claims a free name first holds it, and only they can change, sell or give it away.

A page of your own

Photo, name, a short bio and your links in the order you choose. Share one link everywhere.

Shown in other apps

Apps on Lattice read the same page, so you set your name and photo once instead of in every app.

Handles get taken back. Pages disappear.

How it works

1

Find your name

Search for free. You'll see whether it's available, for sale by its owner, or taken, with close alternatives.

2

Add Selfhold

A free wallet extension for desktop Chrome. It keeps the key that proves the name is yours, on your computer, and asks you before anything is sent.

3

Claim it and set up your page

Approve once and the name is yours. Then add your photo, bio and links.

Pass it on when you want to

Because the name is yours, you can give it to anyone or list it at your own price. A buyer pays you directly, in the same step that moves the name. Moniker takes nothing.

For developers

Show who someone is with <moniker-name>: their photo, name and @handle, linked to their page, read and checked from the network. No account system to build, and no profile database to keep.

import { createMoniker, defineMonikerName } from
  "https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/adalinxx/moniker-sdk@v0.1.2/src/browser.mjs";

const moniker = createMoniker();
defineMonikerName(moniker);

// <moniker-name address="bafyrei…" size="24"></moniker-name>
const who = await moniker.resolve("@rae");   // the holder's address

Questions

Why do I need a wallet extension?

Your name belongs to a key, not to an account on our servers. Selfhold keeps that key on your computer and shows you each change before you approve it. Moniker never sees the key.

Is this crypto? Do I need to buy anything?

Moniker runs on Lattice, an open network. During the trial a free name costs nothing and there's no network fee, so you don't need any coins. Names someone is selling cost what their owner asks.

Can I use it on my phone?

You can search for names and view pages on any device. Claiming and editing need Selfhold, which runs in desktop Chrome. On a phone, Moniker gives you a link to finish on a computer.

Do I have to renew it? Can Moniker take it back?

No renewals. Moniker has no way to take, rename or reassign a name. It stays yours until you sell it, give it away or release it.

What if I lose my computer?

Write down Selfhold's recovery phrase, or keep its encrypted backup, when you set it up. With it you can restore your key, and your name, on another computer. Without it nobody can recover the name, including us.

Someone already holds my name or brand. Can I get it?

Only from them. If they've listed it, you can buy it at their price. Otherwise you can ask them. There's no dispute process that takes a name away.

What does "testnet trial" mean for my name?

Moniker currently runs on Lattice's test network so it can be tried for free. Test networks can be reset, so treat trial names as a preview, not a permanent record.

Does a handle prove who someone is?

It proves the page belongs to whoever holds @name. It doesn't prove they're the person or brand the name suggests.
